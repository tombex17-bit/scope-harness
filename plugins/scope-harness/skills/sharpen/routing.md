# Routing table

| Task class | Examples | Model | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lookup or mechanical | Rename, format, explain a function, factual answer | haiku | low |
| Contained change | One-file bug fix, small feature, summary of one source | sonnet | low or medium |
| Multi-step work | Multi-file feature, refactor, multi-source research | sonnet | high |
| Ambiguous or high-stakes | Unclear root cause, architecture, security, migration | opus | high |
| Novel and hard | New algorithm, deep trade-offs after a failed attempt | opus | xhigh |

Rules: never `max` unless asked; escalate one step only after a real failure;
reserve opus for the last two rows (scarcest on a Pro plan).
